# Dr Adam Nulty tooth libraries

The tooth sets in this folder are made from Dr Adam Nulty's Meshmixer tooth libraries (A01 and F01
teeth, and the A01 pontics), included in Pare 3D with his permission. Credit: Dr Adam Nulty.
They are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International
(CC BY-NC-ND 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/

They are converted to Pare 3D's compact tooth-set format (src/lib/toothLibrary.ts) by
tests/tools/buildNultyLibrary.tool.ts: each tooth simplified to within 0.01 mm of the original
surface and stored in its place in the arch. The pictures (*.png) are drawn from the sets by the same tool.

The permission covers Pare 3D. Anyone redistributing these files outside Pare 3D should check the
licence and Dr Nulty's own terms.
